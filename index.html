01 / ABOUT

Broad electrical engineering foundation. Focused technical depth.

From semiconductor device simulation to integrated circuits, embedded hardware, electrical services and computational modeling.

I graduated with a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Bangladesh. My engineering work spans analog and digital electronics, circuit and CMOS layout design, microcontrollers and embedded applications, electrical wiring and power-system-related analysis.

Semiconductor research is a particular area of depth: I studied double-channel metal-oxide source-gated thin-film transistors using Silvaco ATHENA and ATLAS. Beyond research, I developed and documented collaborative hardware, simulation and software projects across EEE.
